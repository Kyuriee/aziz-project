# Photo page — surface brief

Mode: Experience. Visitor: brand/agency/recruiter judging the photography; success = remembers the images, opens a brand.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Photo page is a table of prints, one brand per colour world; scroll deals the prints, no masonry grid, no filter bar.
OWN-WORLD: Brand palette as ground (bg/fg/accent from `brands[].palette`), giant outline brand name behind, prints with a fg-coloured border and soft offset shadow, tiny tick row and one round arrow as the only chrome.
STORY: Visitor lands on the first brand's stack, scrolls to throw prints one by one, the ground colour changes brand by brand, taps a stack to see full-screen, uses the right rail to jump brands.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Chapter 1 (Tzuyu) full screen: dark teal ground, outline name drifting behind, centred pile of 3+ tilted prints at ~72svh tall, ticks bottom-left, arrow button bottom-right, scroll cue bottom-centre, brand dots on the right edge.
FORM: Pinned scroll-scrubbed print stack per brand (surface structure 5 of 7, seed:08a7cdcdf89e5d55); no catalog challengers (roll degraded).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
